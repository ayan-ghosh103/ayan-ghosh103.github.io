UBER · PRODUCT · EXPERIMENTATION · PRODUCT DELIVERY

UberMe Vouchers

Led end-to-end experimentation for a peer-to-peer voucher product, partnering with Product and Engineering to identify opportunities, formulate hypotheses, define success and guardrail metrics, design experiments and translate evidence into product decisions.

Analysed the voucher journey across sharing, claiming and redemption to identify friction and low-intent behaviour. A representative intervention combined recipient-selection improvements with extending voucher time-to-live from 24 to 48 hours.

As the Data Science point of contact for the global rollout strategy, I worked with Product and Engineering to evaluate rollout risks, monitor potential cannibalization and use experiment and customer evidence to support scaling decisions across markets.

30 → 42% claim rate · 22 → 37% redemption rate · progressive 20% → 50% → 100% rollout
02
UBER · CAUSAL INFERENCE · INCREMENTALITY · VOUCHER PORTFOLIO

Measuring the incremental value of vouchers

Led incrementality analyses across different voucher types within the broader voucher portfolio. The objective was to distinguish genuine incremental activity from behaviour that would have occurred without the voucher, while accounting for underlying trends and potential substitution.

Applied and triangulated Difference-in-Differences, staggered DiD, Synthetic Control and TMLE, using future-treated groups and holdout-based approaches where appropriate. Findings were used to inform investment decisions, prioritisation and subsequent voucher initiatives.

25–36% incremental Trips · 40–52% incremental Gross Bookings

MCAFEE · MACHINE LEARNING · PERSONALISATION · RECOMMENDATIONS

Personalised product recommendations & customer segmentation

Developed customer segmentation and personalised product recommendation models to identify behavioural patterns across customer groups and improve product relevance, with the objective of reducing subscription churn.

Built the ML workflow using MLflow and applied uplift modelling to identify customers most likely to respond incrementally to retention and engagement interventions — moving beyond predicting who would respond to identifying who could be influenced by an intervention.

Extended the customer-level analysis into dynamic subscription pricing by studying segment-level behaviour and price sensitivity, connecting ML-driven customer understanding with commercial decisions around conversion and pricing.

Segmentation + recommendations + uplift modelling · churn reduction objective · MLflow workflow
