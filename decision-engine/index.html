04 · METHOD & PRODUCTION PATH

Evidence in. Policy out.

Natural-language intake

Extract population, costs, value, budget, capacity and objective from a free-form business brief. Missing or ambiguous fields become explicit questions instead of hidden assumptions.

Prediction

Use an existing propensity or risk score to estimate who is likely to experience the outcome. Likelihood alone is not used as proof of incremental impact.

Incrementality

The demo uses synthetic heterogeneous treatment effects. A production version can consume CATE/uplift estimates produced by a validated causal pipeline.

Economic policy

Expected net value is incremental effect × outcome value − intervention cost, subject to budget and operational capacity.

Policy evaluation

Compare target-everyone, propensity, uplift and economic policies on incremental outcomes, spend, net value and ROI rather than model metrics alone.

Production extension

Ingest a customer-level CSV/model output, apply guardrails, estimate policy value with appropriate causal methods, and export the resulting targeting policy and decision memo.

Key idea: the engine is not an LLM that invents a business case. It is a structured decision system with a natural-language intake layer. It extracts what is stated, asks for what is missing, and makes every assumption visible.
